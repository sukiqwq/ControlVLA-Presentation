Presenter notes and notation

This diagram is an architectural abstraction for presentations. It emphasizes condition injection and action updates while omitting Transformer details such as self-attention, residual connections, normalization, and feed-forward networks. “Attention” denotes the network's conditional interaction module. Stage 1 uses text-conditioned diffusion as a teaching baseline, Stage 2 introduces the paper's multimodal VLA, and Stage 3 adds object-centric representations.

At is the future action sequence predicted at environment time t. H is the action horizon, d is the feature dimension, and superscript k is the diffusion-noise level. The initial AtK is Gaussian noise; intermediate Atk remains noisy. Ot denotes observation history, where each observation contains RGB, language, and robot proprioception. Observation conditions are not noised or denoised.

Here OCR means Object-centric Representations, not optical character recognition. Both attention branches share the same Q, compute softmax independently, and add their outputs in feature space before the remaining denoising network. They do not add two action trajectories. Wz and Bz abbreviate the new K/V projection. At zero initialization, Vz = 0, so the new branch contributes zero. The “fine-tuned” state only demonstrates that the branch has begun contributing; it does not imply that the backbone is frozen.

The sampling panel shows deterministic DDIM (η = 0). ᾱk is the cumulative signal-retention coefficient. The predicted noise ε̂ first estimates the clean action, then computes the action at a lower noise level. The paper states that inference uses DDIM; this page uses its deterministic form for explanation. The slider only controls the synthetic curve's noise level, and the zero-initialization toggle only illustrates the branch contribution.
